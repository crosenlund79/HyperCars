- **Game title:** HyperCars (user)
- **Godot version (pin minor, e.g. 4.x):** 4.7.x — developed and tested with 4.7.2-stable, GL Compatibility renderer (user, 2026-11-14)
- **Premise / core player loop:** Undecided
- **Perspective / art direction:** **Approved 2026-11-14 (user, "HyperCars — Approved Visual Direction").** Top-down view. Original, modern, hand-painted HyperCars identity; do **not** reproduce the original Super Cars II pixel art.
  - **Style reference:** the user's own *Fluffy Bears of Doom* drawings in `docs/art_refrence/` (folder name as committed): `bomb`, `chair`, `clown`, `duck`, `effects`, `fluff`, `powerups`, `tiles` (`.png`).
  - **Study only their artistic characteristics:** hand-painted textures, soft brushwork and organic outlines; slightly imperfect, asymmetrical shapes with personality; soft shading, highlights and subtle surface texture; their colour choices, proportions and playful character. **Never recreate or incorporate the depicted objects** (bomb, furniture, bear, chest, clown, duck, etc.).
  - **Applies to** original HyperCars artwork: top-down cars, tracks, terrain, obstacles, weapons, interface elements and other assets as required.
  - **Asset rules:** `template_` graphics must be proper, visually appealing art, not programmer placeholders; all assets stay stylistically consistent so the user's drawings can replace them one at a time; user artwork has priority and missing custom assets fall back to Claude's templates through the existing `custom/` override contract (`assets/README.md`), including replacement in exported Windows/Linux games without rebuilding; consistent dimensions, transparency, alignment and animation/sprite-sheet layouts must be defined; create only assets needed for approved iterations.
- **Confirmed mechanics and constraints:** Gameplay remains mechanically faithful to Super Cars II (1991, Amiga); graphics follow the approved visual direction above (user, 2026-11-14). Controls as confirmed by the user from the original manual (2026-11-14): joystick left/right steer; two control modes — *Fire to Accelerate* (fire held accelerates, releasing fire brakes automatically) and *Fire to Brake* (automatic acceleration, fire held applies stronger braking); joystick up/down fire front/rear weapons, never brake. All physics values are provisional until calibrated against the original. **M1 handling is the approved baseline** (user, 2026-11-14, M1.1+M2 brief): any change to driving physics needs user approval. Original race rule reported by the manual and applied where relevant: the finishing order reverses the next race's grid (winner starts last). **Recurring rivals** (user, 2026-11-14, M2.1): racer ids 1–9 are fixed identities in every race and session — Victor "The Professor", Rocco "Mad Dog", Ellie "Sparrow", Hank "The Wall", Maya "Ghost", Dante "Showboat", Greta "Clockwork", Finn "Wildcard", Nina "The Hunter" (`src/ai/rival_roster.gd`). **All nine personalities** (M2.1 pilot + M2.3, user-approved descriptions) live in `src/ai/personalities/`; Victor's latest tuning is the user's commit 8d58ccd. **Combat (M3)**, verified from the original manual PDF (Gremlin 1991, en/de/fr/it, archive.org copy): one FRONT and one REAR weapon type per race, fired with joystick Forward / Backwards; front missiles fly straight until they hit something and can be dodged; rear missiles the same backwards; homing missiles home on the nearest car; the super missile rotates round its car destroying anything too close; mines explode after a few seconds destroying anything close; weapons destroy cars. User decisions (2026-11-14): fixed loadout front missiles + rear mines for everyone (no shop yet); a destroyed car is wrecked on the spot for a while, then continues. Weapons ON / No Guns is a pre-race option for racing and spectating. All combat numbers provisional (`src/combat/default_weapon_rules.tres`). **Race lengths** (user, 2026-11-14): 3 / 7 / 20 / 50 laps, default 3, chosen before racing or spectating (title LAPS button, saved; Tab on the grid panel) and kept for the whole spectator session; each spectator race records its length. The spectator safety net only classifies cars after 60 s with no progress by any unfinished car (no time limit on long races).

  - `src/driving/` — `CarModel` (pure deterministic driving model; symmetric car-to-car contact), `DrivingParams` (provisional tunables, `default_driving_params.tres`), `Car` scene (physics body, wall and car contacts).

  - `src/ai/` — `RacingLine` (line + speed profile from shared driving params), `AiDriver` (player-equivalent inputs: digital steer + fire; seeded personality decisions), `AiParams` (provisional; neutral `default_ai_params.tres`, pilot personalities in `personalities/`), `RivalRoster` (fixed identities).

  - `src/controls/` — `PlayerControls` (input actions, digital joystick reading). `src/camera/` — `FollowCamera`. `src/ui/` — `Hud`.
