  - **Style reference:** the user's own *Fluffy Bears of Doom* drawings in `docs/art_refrence/` (folder name as committed): `bomb`, `chair`, `clown`, `duck`, `effects`, `fluff`, `powerups`, `tiles` (`.png`).

- **Confirmed mechanics and constraints:** Gameplay remains mechanically faithful to Super Cars II (1991, Amiga); graphics follow the approved visual direction above (user, 2026-11-14). Controls as confirmed by the user from the original manual (2026-11-14): joystick left/right steer; two control modes — *Fire to Accelerate* (fire held accelerates, releasing fire brakes automatically) and *Fire to Brake* (automatic acceleration, fire held applies stronger braking); joystick up/down fire front/rear weapons, never brake. All physics values are provisional until calibrated against the original. **M1 handling is the approved baseline** (user, 2026-11-14, M1.1+M2 brief): any change to driving physics needs user approval. Original race rule reported by the manual and applied where relevant: the finishing order reverses the next race's grid (winner starts last). **Recurring rivals** (user, 2026-11-14, M2.1): racer ids 1–9 are fixed identities in every race and session — Victor "The Professor", Rocco "Mad Dog", Ellie "Sparrow", Hank "The Wall", Maya "Ghost", Dante "Showboat", Greta "Clockwork", Finn "Wildcard", Nina "The Hunter" (`src/ai/rival_roster.gd`). **All nine personalities** (M2.1 pilot + M2.3, user-approved descriptions) live in `src/ai/personalities/`; Victor's latest tuning is the user's commit 8d58ccd. **Combat (M3)**, verified from the original manual PDF (Gremlin 1991, en/de/fr/it, archive.org copy): one FRONT and one REAR weapon type per race, fired with joystick Forward / Backwards; front missiles fly straight until they hit something and can be dodged; rear missiles the same backwards; homing missiles home on the nearest car; the super missile rotates round its car destroying anything too close; mines explode after a few seconds destroying anything close; weapons destroy cars. User decisions (2026-11-14): fixed loadout front missiles + rear mines for everyone (no shop yet); a destroyed car is wrecked on the spot for a while, then continues. Weapons ON / No Guns is a pre-race option for racing and spectating. All combat numbers provisional (`src/combat/default_weapon_rules.tres`). **M3.5 combat styles** (user, 2026-11-15): *Classic* = the M3 rules, shared 0.6 s cooldown and M3 AI decisions, unchanged; *Tactical* = front slot 1.5 s, rear slot 3 s, independent slot cooldowns plus a brief shared firing lock (0.35 s, provisional), all configurable in `WeaponRules`. No Guns stays independent of the style. Each car carries its own loadout (one front + one rear weapon type); slots (HyperCars decision): front = front / homing / super missiles, rear = rear missiles / mines. **Garage races (M3.5)**: verified from the manual — starting money, prize money by finishing position (top five only), buy / sell after a race (selling pays less), FRONT / REAR choice, two weapon types per race, quantity limited only by money. HyperCars choices (provisional, `src/garage/default_garage_rules.tres`): fixed prices (front / rear missile $40, homing $90, super $150, mine $30; original prices fluctuated — not implemented), sell = 50 %, start $300 + 5 front missiles + 5 mines equipped, prizes $500 / 350 / 250 / 150 / 100, max 99 rounds per type; money and weapons last for the game session (not saved); quick races use a standard loadout and never touch the garage; spectating never touches it. Tactical rivals carry fixed personal loadouts of equal shop value ($600, `src/garage/rival_armoury.gd`); the player's Tactical quick-race loadout has the same value; Classic keeps the M3 loadout for everyone. **Tactical AI (M3.5)**: `AiDriver.decide_weapons_tactical` judges each mounted weapon from real target position, distance, direction and relative speed (straight / rear shot prediction, homing lock on the nearest car ahead, cars around the super-missile orbit, mine timing), ammunition, slot readiness, pressure and laps left; personalities only shape thresholds, patience, eagerness and ammo saving (new neutral-default traits `tactical_aim_time`, `rear_eagerness`, `super_min_targets`, `brilliance_rate`). Classic AI decisions are unchanged. **Achievements & Living Legends (M3.5, a HyperCars addition, not in the original)**: the 28 proposed achievements plus "The Eternal Contender" (a rival reaches 25 podiums without a win in between) and "The Curse Is Broken" (a rival wins after 50+ consecutive winless races); 6 hidden until unlocked. They count completed races in normal racing and spectating at any simulation speed; rival feats count whether you race or watch; each unlocks once, saved in `user://achievements.cfg` with per-rival careers (races, wins, podiums, finishing-position counts, winless streak, podiums since last win, longest winless run); no race logs, nothing retroactive. A normal race becomes final when every car is in, or when you leave the results (cars still running are placed in running order). Toast on unlock; title ACHIEVEMENTS catalogue with rival careers; RESET ACHIEVEMENTS asks for confirmation and erases achievements, progress and rival careers. **Track theme variants** (user, 2026-11-15): three visual variants with generic internal names — Variant 1 = the existing green baseline ("Green Valley"), Variant 2 ("Heather Moor": moorland ground, stone-wall barrier, rowan / heather / boulder props) and Variant 3 ("Snow Pass": snow ground, dusted road, blue-white barrier, snowy pine / drift / ice-rock props). The original Super Cars II had grass, snow and rocks-and-heather circuits (manual); HyperCars art is original. Display names are thematic, code and folders are not (`variant_N`). Identical layout, collision, physics and race rules on every variant (snow does not change grip). Chosen with the title TRACK button (saved), used for racing and spectating. **Two-player races (M4, user-approved provisional rules, 2026-11-15)**: exactly 10 cars — Player 1 (racer 0) and Player 2 (racer 10) plus eight rivals; one rival sits out each race in a fixed rotation (Victor, Rocco, … Nina, repeat; the returning rival takes the leaving rival's reversed-grid slot); first grid: rivals in id order, then P2, P1 last; both humans must finish before the results, with a safety net (once anyone has finished, a human with no progress for `human_stuck_timeout` = 30 s, provisional, releases the results; an active player is never cut short); same physics, combat and AI; each human on their own device-bound actions (`p1_` / `p2_`); grid-panel control mode applies to both; garage and achievements are off (no saves or rival careers touched); no spectating from a two-player session. **Race lengths** (user, 2026-11-14): 3 / 7 / 20 / 50 laps, default 3, chosen before racing or spectating (title LAPS button, saved; Tab on the grid panel) and kept for the whole spectator session; each spectator race records its length. The spectator safety net only classifies cars after 60 s with no progress by any unfinished car (no time limit on long races).
