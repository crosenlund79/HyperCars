User decisions (2026-11-14): fixed loadout front missiles + rear mines for everyone (no shop yet); a destroyed car is wrecked on the spot for a while, then continues. No Guns stays independent of the style. Each car carries its own loadout (one front + one rear weapon type); slots (HyperCars decision): front = front / homing / super missiles, rear = rear missiles / mines. **Garage races (M3.5)**: verified from the manual — starting money, prize money by finishing position (top five only), buy / sell after a race (selling pays less), FRONT / REAR choice, two weapon types per race, quantity limited only by money. HyperCars choices (provisional, `src/garage/default_garage_rules.tres`): fixed prices (front / rear missile $40, homing $90, super $150, mine $30; original prices fluctuated — not implemented), sell = 50 %, start $300 + 5 front missiles + 5 mines equipped, prizes $500 / 350 / 250 / 150 / 100, max 99 rounds per type; money and weapons last for the game session (not saved); quick races use a standard loadout and never touch the garage; spectating never touches it. Tactical rivals carry fixed personal loadouts of equal shop value ($600, `src/garage/rival_armoury.gd`); the player's Tactical quick-race loadout has the same value; Classic keeps the M3 loadout for everyone. **Tactical AI (M3.5)**: `AiDriver.decide_weapons_tactical` judges each mounted weapon from real target position, distance, direction and relative speed (straight / rear shot prediction, homing lock on the nearest car ahead, cars around the super-missile orbit, mine timing), ammunition, slot readiness, pressure and laps left; personalities only shape thresholds, patience, eagerness and ammo saving (new neutral-default traits `tactical_aim_time`, `rear_eagerness`, `super_min_targets`, `brilliance_rate`). Classic AI decisions are unchanged. **Achievements & Living Legends (M3.5, a HyperCars addition, not in the original)**: the 28 proposed achievements plus "The Eternal Contender" (a rival reaches 25 podiums without a win in between) and "The Curse Is Broken" (a rival wins after 50+ consecutive winless races); 6 hidden until unlocked. Identical layout, collision, physics and race rules on every variant (snow does not change grip). Chosen with the title TRACK button (saved), used for racing and spectating. **Two-player races (M4, user-approved provisional rules, 2026-11-15)**: exactly 10 cars — Player 1 (racer 0) and Player 2 (racer 10) plus eight rivals; one rival sits out each race in a fixed rotation (Victor, Rocco, … Nina, repeat; the returning rival takes the leaving rival's reversed-grid slot); first grid: rivals in id order, then P2, P1 last; both humans must finish before the results, with a safety net (once anyone has finished, a human with no progress for `human_stuck_timeout` = 30 s, provisional, releases the results; an active player is never cut short); same physics, combat and AI; each human on their own device-bound actions (`p1_` / `p2_`); grid-panel control mode applies to both; garage and achievements are off (no saves or rival careers touched); no spectating from a two-player session. **Couch split-screen (M4 Stage 2)**: Race → TWO PLAYERS: SPLIT SCREEN → press-a-button join (Cross / A per controller, Space for one keyboard player); side-by-side views (two 640×720 halves of 16:9, rendered at the window's pixel density), each with its own rigid follow camera (zoom 0.85) and HUD (position, lap, time, weapons, cooldowns, DESTROYED, compact standings in the view's bottom outer corner since P1); shared grid panel, banners and results; P1 gold / P2 blue rows and car markers (P1 polish); positional sound heard from between the two cars; lighting off by default (L still toggles). **LAN races (M5.1, prototype)**: host-authoritative over ENet (UDP port 24680, IPv4, same network); Race → TWO PLAYERS: LAN — one computer HOSTS (shows its address and waits), the other types that address and JOINS; both land on the grid panel (host's track, laps, weapons and style; each player picks their own control mode), the client presses Fire to be ready, the host starts; each computer follows its own car with its own HUD; host Fire after the results starts the next race (reversed grid + rival rotation); a disconnect ends the race with a CONNECTION LOST message and Fire / Esc returns to the menu (no AI takeover); a race in progress refuses new players; garage and achievements are off. Client view is interpolated ~4 ticks behind the newest snapshot; no prediction (client steering feels one network round trip late). **Online (M5.2)**: the same flow over the internet — the joiner types the host's public IPv4 address (optionally `:port`; default UDP 24680), the host forwards that UDP port and allows it in the firewall (`docs/NETWORK_PLAY.md`, incl. CGNAT); WELCOME / HELLO version handshake (protocol 2 + build string) in both directions, a silent visitor is dropped after 8 s, ENet timeouts 5–10 s; every message is checked for sender, shape and state before use. **Client prediction (M5.2 Stage 2)**: the client drives its own car at once with the unchanged Car / CarModel (walls only; other cars, contacts, weapons, damage and results stay host-authoritative); snapshots carry the last input tick the host applied, the host's state for that tick is compared with the client's record and the difference corrected (30 % per snapshot when small, at once above 64 px or 0.6 rad; corrections shift the recorded history so they apply once); no prediction while wrecked; remote cars stay interpolated. **Reliability (M5.2 Stage 3)**: small connection line bottom-right in network races ("PING n ms", red "CONNECTION PROBLEMS" after 1.5 s of silence or > 5 % loss); Fire only readies (a control-mode change un-readies); HOST / JOIN ignored while a session is active; a visitor leaving the lobby keeps the host waiting; disconnects in lobby, race or results end cleanly; consecutive sessions start fresh. **In-race Esc menu** (user, 2026-11-16): Esc (any player's Back) during a race opens RESUME RACE / LEAVE RACE; single-player, spectator and couch races pause (resuming is bit-identical to never pausing); a network race is never paused (this computer's car brakes, weapons off) and leaving disconnects; Esc on the grid panel still leaves; an unfinished garage race pays nothing and uses no rounds. **Race lengths** (user, 2026-11-14): 3 / 7 / 20 / 50 laps, default 3, chosen before racing or spectating (title LAPS button, saved; Tab on the grid panel) and kept for the whole spectator session; each spectator race records its length. The spectator safety net only classifies cars after 60 s with no progress by any unfinished car (no time limit on long races).
